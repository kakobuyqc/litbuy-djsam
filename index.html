03 / Litbuy shopping guide

A better list starts
with better questions.

A useful Litbuy product spreadsheet needs more than a photo and a price. Keep enough information to compare similar items without repeatedly starting your research from scratch.

These category shortcuts take you to CAFANS. Check the original seller link and your chosen shopping service’s support before attempting an order.

Start your category research ↗

Choose one category and one purpose.

Begin with a specific need: everyday sneakers, a layering hoodie, a lightweight jacket or a simple accessory. Narrow comparisons are easier to evaluate.

Record the exact listing and variation.

Save the product URL, seller, color, size, listed material and date checked. Similar photographs can appear on listings with different specifications.

Compare measurements and evidence.

Review size charts and available detail photos. Compare clothing measurements with a garment you own, and check footwear sizing information for the exact listing.

Estimate the full cost before deciding.

Consider the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable import taxes. Recheck the current terms before payment.

What belongs in your spreadsheet?

Suggested fields for comparing shoes, clothing and accessories.
Product groupRecord these detailsCheck before choosing
Shoes Model reference, size system, color, seller URL Listing-specific sizing, construction photos and available measurements.
Hoodies & T-shirts Chest, shoulder, length, sleeve, stated fabric How the measurements compare with a garment that already fits.
Jackets & sets Layering allowance, closure, lining, included pieces Whether each part of a set has its own size chart.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening, stretch Measurement method and whether the waist is fixed or adjustable.
Headwear & jewelry Circumference or length, dimensions, stated material Fit range, fastening, care guidance and seller disclosures.

Keep “verified” and “unknown” details separate. A missing specification should remain a question until you have evidence.

06 / Clear answers

Before you
follow a find.

Practical answers for using a product list with more context and fewer assumptions.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, categories, sizes and comparison notes. A list is a starting point for research; it does not verify a seller, authenticate a product or guarantee availability.

How do I use a Litbuy product spreadsheet?

Choose a category, shortlist relevant items, check the current product page, compare measurements and calculate the total expected cost. Record the date you checked each listing so older notes are easy to identify.

Is this the official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping research guide. It is not operated by or affiliated with Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS.

Can I assume a CAFANS link works with Litbuy?

No. A CAFANS category link is a browsing shortcut. Verify the original seller URL and whether your chosen shopping service supports it before attempting an order.

Does a spreadsheet verify authenticity or quality?

No. Product photos and spreadsheet entries do not establish authenticity, authorization or manufacturing quality. Review the seller’s evidence, product details and applicable return terms independently.

Which costs should I compare before ordering?

Compare the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable import taxes. The final amount depends on the seller, service, destination and parcel details.
